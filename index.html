Mailmatta

For this project, my biggest task was making the character movement, taking inspiration from 3D Sonic Games and Landfall's Haste. Instead of relying on Unreal's physics engine, I calculated all of the physics of the player by manipulating its velocity directly, resulting in highly customizable, arcadey physics. Consequently, my vector math improved greatly and I have a newfound understanding of simulated physics.

About Me
